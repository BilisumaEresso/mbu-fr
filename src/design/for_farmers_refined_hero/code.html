How to Join Meki Batu Union

1

Form a Primary Cooperative

Organize local farmers into a legally registered primary cooperative under local agricultural directives.

2

Submit Application

Provide the union with your registration documents, member roster, and formal request for union affiliation.

3

Assessment & Onboarding

Undergo our standard capacity assessment followed by initial training sessions to integrate into our supply chain.
